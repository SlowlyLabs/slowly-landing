How it works

Three steps. That's the product.

01

Write your sentence

One question, one input. "How do you want to feel?" Calmer. Stronger. Rested. Present. Your words become the app's compass.

02

Open it once a day

The coach gives you one verb. Walk. Sleep early. Eat slowly. One coach line, one button. The whole daily experience fits on one screen.

03

Sundays, look back

A weekly recap, written like a letter. "You said you wanted to feel calmer. This week, you did." Then plan the next.
